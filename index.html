How prices get updated

This page can't reach eBay on its own — published pages are sandboxed and can't call outside sites. So pricing runs through Claude instead, and the ledger is the record it writes to.

To add a purchase

Tell Claude in chat: "bought 6 Prismatic Evolutions ETBs on March 4, paid $49.99 each at Costco." It writes the lot straight into this ledger.

To re-price everything

Say run it. Claude pulls the most recent eBay sold listings for each lot, averages the top three, and writes the result back here with the individual sales shown as receipts.

Why the average of three

One sale is noise — a bad photo or a Tuesday-night auction can land 20% under the market. Three recent sales smooths that without going so far back that the number is stale.
